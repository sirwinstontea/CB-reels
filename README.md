# CB reels

Work in progress. CB reels turns a coloring-book page into a realistic short video of it being colored by hand with alcohol markers, ready to post as an Instagram reel.

It's built for people who sell coloring books online as a side hustle and need a steady stream of video content to market them.

## How it works
- An agentic AI pipeline: several AI agents plan the coloring order, generate the frames and check each shot.
- Real desk and hand references, so the scene looks filmed rather than generated.
- Strokes are matched to reference videos of real marker coloring: smooth, blended alcohol-marker strokes that stay inside the lines, with paper texture, shading and gradients.
- Every post carries an AI label.
